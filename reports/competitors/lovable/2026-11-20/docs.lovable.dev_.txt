Copy page Copy page Lovable lets individuals and teams build production-grade web applications using natural language. You describe what you want to build, and Lovable generates a working application that includes frontend, backend, database, authentication, and integrations, all backed by editable code. Lovable supports the full lifecycle of modern product development, from early exploration and prototyping to deployment and ongoing operation. Applications are built inside shared workspaces , where a group of people builds from one plan and a shared pool of credits, each working on one or more of their own projects. Each project represents a single application and produces a codebase that can be synced to GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket with Git sync and integrated into existing engineering workflows. The platform is built to help people move faster while maintaining ownership, security, and operational control. You can start for free, and plans are priced by credits , not by projects or seats. See Subscription plans . Start building for free Create a free account with email, Google, GitHub, or Apple, and start building immediately. Quick start Build and publish your first app in about ten minutes. Browse Lovable templates Start from a ready-made app you can remix on any plan. Learn in the Lovable Academy Free video walkthroughs and a guided learning path from the Lovable team. Core concepts Four terms come up everywhere in Lovable: Account : Your personal identity on Lovable. You log in with one account and use it across every workspace you belong to. Workspace : Where you and your team work. A workspace holds projects, members, billing, and a shared pool of credits. Project : One app. Each project holds its project chat, preview, code, and version history, and lives in exactly one workspace. Chats : Conversations with Lovable outside your projects, for thinking through ideas, asking about your apps, and working with connected tools. When something needs building, a chat sends the work to a project. Why use Lovable Lovable makes it easier to go from an idea to working software. Faster iteration Move from idea to working software by building through natural language instead of starting from a blank codebase. Collaboration when you need it Work solo or with a team. Lovable supports shared workspaces so people across roles can contribute in one place when collaboration matters. Code ownership and flexibility Keep full ownership of your code and integrate Lovable into existing workflows. Built with enterprise needs in mind Security, privacy, and governance capabilities are built into the platform from the start.
